# Robust-statistics rationale
rationale:
  - "Naive mean+std baseline FAILS detection on synthetic test fixture: with 23% of (hour=12, dow=Friday) bucket spiked 10×, both mean and std are dragged up by the very spikes we want to detect — z-score of spiked bar drops below 2.5σ. Proven analytically (mean ≈ 0.0025 with vs ≈ 0.001 quiet, std ≈ 0.003) and empirically by initial test failures."
  - "Robust statistics (median + 1.4826*MAD) tolerates up to ~50% contamination. For the same fixture: median range stays at the quiet baseline (~0.0008), MAD-based scale stays small, z-score for spike ≈ 12 (well above threshold). This is why CONTEXT D-10 says '2.5σ' but Plan 04 implements MAD-based scale: the σ refers to the dispersion measure, and robust σ is the statistically-correct choice when the bucket itself is contaminated."

## What was built

Plan 04 ships the empirical risk-calendar pipeline that closes SESS-03. Three core functions plus a thin CLI driver hook:

1. **`detect_blackout_bars`** — robust outlier detection on bar realized range, per-(hour, dow) bucket. The robustness choice is decisive: mean+std fails analytically when the spike pattern represents >10% of its bucket (which 1st-Friday-NFP does for the (12:00, Friday) bucket). Median + 1.4826*MAD tolerates up to ~50% contamination and recovers ≥90% of the synthetic injection on 4yr H1 corpora.

2. **`cluster_into_patterns`** — turns a flat list of detected timestamps into parametric recurring rules: wom (Nth weekday of month), dow (every weekday), mom (Nth day of month), dates fallback for the unmatched residual. Each pattern carries `affects=[3-letter currency codes]` inferred from the source pair (D-13). Confidence gates require both ≥3 occurrences and ≥50% of expected span coverage to avoid spurious patterns.

3. **`write_risk_calendar`** — ruamel.yaml round-trip with operator-comment preservation. The merge contract is precedence-first: empirical detections override conflicting manual entries on key (pattern, time, affects), but non-conflicting manuals are kept. Operator comments at the document level (e.g. `# manually curated 2026-04-27`) survive across re-runs because ruamel.yaml round-trip mode preserves block-level metadata even when the value is replaced.

4. **`detect_and_write_risk_calendar`** + CLI wiring — the end-to-end pass that the operator invokes. Pools H1 detections across the unique pairs in the active matrix, unions affects per-pattern across contributing pairs, writes the merged YAML.
