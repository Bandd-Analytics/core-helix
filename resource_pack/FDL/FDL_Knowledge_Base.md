## Core Strategy: Tide Shift Pattern

### Overview

The **Tide Shift Pattern** is FDL's flagship strategy - a high-probability setup with ideal risk/reward (typically 4:1). It's not a trend reversal strategy, but rather a correction move that occurs after a strong trend, bringing price back to the 200EMA to stabilize.

### Key Characteristics

- **Frequency**: Develops 2-3 times per week
- **Timeframes**: Best on 1H and 4H (day trading focus)
- **Move Size**: Typically 50-70 pips
- **Risk/Reward**: Typically 4:1 or better
- **Win Rate**: High probability when all rules followed

---

## Strategy Components

### 1. Market Structure

**Definition**: Identifying market structure breaks or strong moves making new higher highs or lower lows.

**Purpose**: Points to beginning of potential new cycle (similar to A, B, C, D structure or Elliot Wave).

**Usage**: 
- Identify structure break on primary timeframe (1H/4H)
- Move to 2 standard timeframes higher for trend confirmation
- Example: 15M structure break → check 1H for trend direction

### 2. Trend Direction

**Definition**: Predicted using moving averages (14EMA, 50SMA, 200EMA).

**Rules**:
- All three averages must align
- 14EMA and 50SMA must be FLAT before crossing
- 200EMA is the target/stabilization point

**TradingView**: Search "FDL MA" to auto-populate all three averages.

### 3. Fractals

**Definition**: Market structure patterns that repeat across timeframes.

**Usage**:
- Move to 2 standard timeframes higher than current
- Example: Analyzing 15M → move to 1H for trend direction
- Smaller timeframes = smaller impact and strength

### 4. Support and Resistance

#### Static Support/Resistance
- Key levels where price hesitates
- "Magnetic areas" that attract or repel price
- Sharp moves bounce off them (ping pong reaction)

#### Dynamic Support/Resistance
- Controlled by moving averages
- Changes candle by candle
- Higher moving average = stronger rejection
- 50SMA used as dynamic confluence

### 5. Fibonacci Retracements

**Levels Used**:
- **0.382** (38.2%)
- **0.62** (62%)
- **0.705** (70.5%)
- **0.79** (79%)

**Usage**: Enter on retracement zone with confluence at resistance/support.

## Tide Shift Rules

### Rule 1: 200EMA Touch
- Tide Shift is considered **complete** upon touch of 200EMA
- This is the stabilization point after big trends

### Rule 2: Avoid Double/Triple Tests
- **Avoid** taking setup if 200EMA already tested
- May be forming double bottom or triple bottom
- Wait for fresh setup

### Rule 3: Flat Moving Averages
- Moving averages must be **FLAT** before crossing
- 14EMA and 50SMA must be flat
- This indicates exhaustion in trend direction

### Rule 4: Wait for Retracement
- **Wait** for retracement before entering
- Use Fibonacci levels for entry zones
- Enter with confluence at support/resistance

## Entry Process

### Step-by-Step Entry

1. **Identify Market Structure Break**
   - New higher high or lower low
   - On 1H or 4H timeframe

2. **Confirm Trend Direction**
   - Check moving averages alignment
   - 14EMA, 50SMA, 200EMA must align
   - Move to 2 timeframes higher for confirmation

3. **Wait for Moving Average Cross**
   - 14EMA crosses 50SMA
   - Both must be FLAT before crossing
   - Signals Tide Shift entry opportunity

4. **Move to Lower Timeframe**
   - Switch to 15M (if analyzing 1H)
   - Look for market structure
   - Seek entry on retracement

5. **Find Confluence**
   - Retracement to Fibonacci level (.382, .62, .705, .79)
   - Confluence with resistance (Bearish) or support (Bullish)
   - Preferably both static and dynamic resistance/support

6. **Enter Trade**
   - Entry on 15M timeframe
   - With confluence at Fibonacci retracement
   - Stop loss below/above recent swing

## Exit Process

### Take Profit Strategy

1. **Primary Target**: 200EMA touch
   - Take **partial profit** at 200EMA
   - Move stop to **breakeven** on remaining position
   - This keeps risk out of account

2. **Secondary Target**: Previous highs/lows
   - After 200EMA touch, trend usually continues
   - Target previous highs (Bullish) or lows (Bearish)
   - Allows for continued profit

### Stop Loss

- **Initial**: Below/above recent swing
- **After Partial Profit**: Move to breakeven at 200EMA touch
- **Risk/Reward**: Typically 4:1 or better

## Bullish vs Bearish Tide Shift

### Bullish Tide Shift

**Setup**:
- Price makes lower lows (downtrend)
- Price becomes separated from moving averages
- Double bottom or head and shoulders may form
- 14EMA crosses **above** 50SMA (both flat)
- Entry on retracement with confluence at **support**
- Target: 200EMA, then previous highs

### Bearish Tide Shift

**Setup**:
- Price makes higher highs (uptrend)
- Price becomes separated from moving averages
- Double top or head and shoulders may form
- 14EMA crosses **below** 50SMA (both flat)
- Entry on retracement with confluence at **resistance**
- Target: 200EMA, then previous lows

---

## Risk Management

### Key Principles

1. **4:1 Risk/Reward**: Typical for Tide Shift due to strength
2. **Partial Profit**: Take profit at 200EMA, move stop to breakeven
3. **Confluence Required**: Never enter without multiple confirmations
4. **Flat Averages**: Must wait for averages to be flat before cross

### Why It Works

- High probability due to confluence
- Strong moves (50-70 pips typical)
- Clear rules reduce emotional trading
- Risk management built into strategy

## Timeframe Guidelines

### Primary Timeframes
- **1 Hour (1H)**: Best for day traders
- **4 Hour (4H)**: Alternative, stronger moves
- **15 Minute (15M)**: Entry timeframe after higher timeframe confirmation

### Timeframe Rules
- Smaller timeframes = smaller impact
- Use 2 standard timeframes higher for trend
- 1H/4H best for same-day exits
- Fractal nature means works on any timeframe

## Market Types

### Position Traders
- Hold trades for months/years
- Smaller capital to sustain volatility
- Bigger picture focus

### Swing Traders
- Hold positions 2 days to 1 week
- Capture complete moves
- Fairly bigger position size

### Intraday Traders (FDL Focus)
- Analyze charts during day
- Ride trend for short period
- End day in cash (no overnight positions)
- Sleep with zero worries
- Clear weekly/monthly/annual returns
