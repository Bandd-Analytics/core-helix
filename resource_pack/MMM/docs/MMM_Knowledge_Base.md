## Chart Observations

### Key Chart Patterns and Observations

#### 1. Stop Hunt
- **Definition**: Aggressive move by market maker to trigger stops
- **Characteristics**: 
  - Price sweeps obvious stops beyond a level then reverses
  - Used to identify false breakouts
  - Traps liquidity before the real move
- **Variations**: 
  - Asian Range Stop Hunt
  - Extended Stop Hunt (beyond 25-50 pip range)
- **Location**: Often at HOD/LOD, obvious support/resistance

#### 2. M & W Formations
- **M Formation (Double Top)**:
  - Appears at HOD (High of Day)
  - Used to trap traders and validate retail orders
  - Two peaks suggesting reversal
- **W Formation (Double Bottom)**:
  - Appears at LOD (Low of Day)
  - Used to trap traders and validate retail orders
  - Two troughs suggesting reversal
- **Context**: Part of stop hunt pattern, validates retail orders

#### 3. 3 Day Cycle
- **Definition**: Market maker cycle that repeats over 3 days
- **Characteristics**:
  - Day 1: Setup/accumulation
  - Day 2: Move/execution
  - Day 3: Distribution/reversal
- **Usage**: Helps identify where in the cycle price is

#### 4. Weekly Cycle
- **Definition**: Weekly market maker cycle
- **Characteristics**: Similar to 3-day but over weekly timeframe
- **Usage**: Higher timeframe context for daily trades

#### 5. False Support & Resistance (False S&R)
- **Definition**: Levels that appear to be support/resistance but are actually traps
- **Characteristics**: Price breaks through, triggers stops, then reverses
- **Usage**: Identify trap moves and real levels

#### 6. Half Batman
- **Definition**: Partial batman pattern (incomplete formation)
- **Characteristics**: Similar to M/W but incomplete
- **Usage**: Early warning of potential reversal

#### 7. Hi Lo (High/Low)
- **Definition**: Tracking of daily highs and lows
- **Characteristics**: 
  - HOD (High of Day)
  - LOD (Low of Day)
  - I-HOD (Initial High of Day - set in Asian session)
  - I-LOD (Initial Low of Day - set in Asian session)
- **Usage**: Key reference points for trading zones

#### 8. Trapping Volume
- **Definition**: Volume patterns that indicate market maker accumulation
- **Characteristics**: High volume at consolidation areas
- **Usage**: Identify position building by market makers

#### 9. Wedges
- **Definition**: Wedge patterns (ascending/descending)
- **Characteristics**: Converging trend lines
- **Usage**: Identify potential breakouts or reversals

## Chart Setups

### Components of Chart Analysis

#### 1. Counting
- **Level I**: Basic swing counting (1-2-3)
- **Level II**: Intermediate structure identification
- **Level III**: Advanced wave counting
- **Usage**: Define structure and potential reversal/continuation points

#### 2. Peak Formation
- **Peak Formation High**: Highest point on chart (intraday/intraweek)
- **Peak Formation Low**: Lowest point on chart (intraday/intraweek)
- **Usage**: Identify reversal points, M tops, W bottoms

#### 3. 3 Day Cycle Analysis
- **Intraday Cycle**: Within-day market maker cycle
- **3 Day Cycle**: Multi-day cycle pattern
- **Usage**: Understand market maker timing and positioning

#### 4. Colour-Coded Sessions
- **Asian Session**: 00:30-07:00 GMT (sets I-HOD/I-LOD)
- **European Session**: 07:30-13:00 GMT
- **US Session**: 13:30-20:30 GMT
- **Gap Times**: Transition periods between sessions
- **Usage**: Session-based trading setups

#### 5. EMA's (Exponential Moving Averages)
- **Usage**: Trend identification, entry/exit signals
- **Common Periods**: 14, 21, 50, 200
- **Context**: Used with market maker pattern

#### 6. RSI (Relative Strength Index)
- **Usage**: Divergence patterns, overbought/oversold
- **Context**: Used to identify weakening momentum

#### 7. TDI (Traders Dynamic Index)
- **Developer**: Dean Malone
- **Usage**: Used in conjunction with market maker pattern
- **Context**: Entry/exit signals based on TDI

#### 8. Confluence of Signals
- **Definition**: Multiple indicators/signals aligning
- **Usage**: Higher probability setups when multiple factors align
- **Components**: 
  - Chart patterns
  - Indicators
  - Levels
  - Sessions

## Trend Assessment

### Basic Trend Analysis

#### 1. Market Maker Trend
- **Definition**: The real trend of the market, different from retail perception
- **Characteristics**: 
  - May differ from apparent retail trend
  - Based on market maker positioning
  - True direction of price movement
- **Usage**: Identify actual market direction vs. perceived direction

#### 2. Trend Identification
- **Uptrend**: Series of higher highs and higher lows
- **Downtrend**: Series of lower highs and lower lows
- **Ranging**: No clear trend, consolidation
- **Usage**: Determine trade direction

#### 3. Trend Reset
- **Definition**: Market maker pullback to book profit, then continues trend
- **Characteristics**: 
  - Temporary pullback in trend direction
  - Entry opportunity in trend direction
  - Not a reversal, continuation expected
- **Usage**: Entry points in trend direction

#### 4. Correction
- **Definition**: Lowering of price in uptrend context (or rise in downtrend)
- **Characteristics**: Temporary move against trend
- **Usage**: Entry opportunity after correction

#### 5. Personal Observations
- **PT Indicator on 5 Minute Chart**: 
  - PT stop dots often against true trend at entry
  - Should change soon after entry
  - Additional entries can be taken on trend confirmation

## Trading and Trade Setups

### Components of a Trading System

#### 1. Rules To Profit
- **Selectivity**: Be extremely selective, trade only high-probability setups
- **Position Sizing**: Trade heavily on highly selected setups
- **Relaxation**: Trading should be relaxing, not stressful
- **Focus**: Make as much as possible from selected moves

#### 2. Fractional Disparity
- **Definition**: Small differences in price movement
- **Usage**: Identify market maker positioning
- **Context**: Used in scanning and analysis

#### 3. Scanning View
- **Purpose**: Identify potential setups across multiple pairs
- **Components**: 
  - Chart patterns
  - Session timing
  - Level identification
  - Trend assessment

#### 4. Putting the Chart Together
- **Steps**:
  1. Identify trend (market maker trend)
  2. Identify key levels (HOD/LOD, I-HOD/I-LOD)
  3. Identify chart patterns (M/W, stop hunts)
  4. Check session timing
  5. Look for confluence
  6. Identify trading zone

#### 5. Strike Zones
- **Definition**: Key areas where setups occur
- **Characteristics**: 
  - Within 15-20 pips of HOD/LOD
  - At session opens
  - At confluence of levels and patterns
- **Usage**: Entry zone identification

#### 6. A Suggested Routine
- **Morning Routine**:
  1. Check daily high/low reset (17:00 ET)
  2. Identify I-HOD/I-LOD from Asian session
  3. Calculate market maker spread
  4. Identify trading zones
  5. Wait for session opens
  6. Look for setups in trading zones

### Trade Setups

#### 1. The Trading Zone
- **Definition**: Area safe to enter trade (15-20 pips from HOD/LOD)
- **Characteristics**: 
  - Defined key zone
  - Within safe range of extremes
  - Often at session opens
- **Usage**: Primary entry zone

#### 2. The Straightaway Trade
- **Definition**: Sustained directional move with little pullback
- **Characteristics**: 
  - One-way move
  - Used to create margin calls
  - Little to no retracement
- **Usage**: Trade in direction of move or on first pullback

#### 3. The 2nd Leg M or W Setup
- **Definition**: Entry on second leg of M/W formation
- **Characteristics**: 
  - M top: Second peak at HOD
  - W bottom: Second trough at LOD
  - Confirmation of reversal
- **Usage**: High-probability reversal entry

#### 4. The 33 Trade
- **Definition**: Three legs in one direction, then three in opposite
- **Characteristics**: 
  - Similar to 22 trade but with 3 legs
  - More extended pattern
  - Higher timeframe pattern
- **Usage**: Multi-day/week setups

#### 5. The 22 Trade
- **Definition**: Two legs in one direction, then two in opposite
- **Characteristics**: 
  - Classic MMM pattern
  - Often at key levels or sessions
  - Uses counting levels
- **Usage**: Session-based setups, level-based entries

### Market Timing

#### Session-Based Trading
- **London Session Open**: 07:30 GMT (08:00 GMT typical)
- **NYC Session Open**: 13:30 GMT (08:00 AM ET)
- **Asian Session**: Sets I-HOD/I-LOD
- **Gap Times**: Transition periods, important for reversals

#### Daily High/Low Reset
- **Time**: 17:00 ET (22:00 GMT)
- **Purpose**: New daily high/low tracking begins
- **Usage**: Reset reference points for next day

## Core Concepts

### Market Maker Objectives
1. **Profit**: Sole goal is to make profit
2. **Price Manipulation**: Only tool is manipulating price
3. **Position Building**: Accumulate positions before moves
4. **Stop Hunting**: Trigger retail stops before real move
5. **Liquidity**: Create liquidity for large orders

### Market Maker Tools
1. **ADR Restriction**: Limited by IMF to ADR range (up to 200 pips/day)
2. **Equity Base**: Large equity base for positioning
3. **Media Control**: Influence market sentiment
4. **Political Influence**: Affect currency through policy

### Key Principles
1. **Beat the Market Maker**: Understand their objectives and tools
2. **Trade with Market Makers**: Not against them
3. **Selectivity**: Only trade high-probability setups
4. **Patience**: Wait for proper setups
5. **Structure**: Understand market structure and cycles
